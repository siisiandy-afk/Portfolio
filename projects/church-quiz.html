THE IDEA

Church Quiz is an interactive, low-latency quiz buzzer and scoring system designed to make competitions exciting, fair, and seamless. It synchronizes multiple contestants' devices with a live projection scoreboard and admin control panel.

THE PROBLEM

Traditional quiz competitions rely on manual verbal buzzers, physical hand-raising, or makeshift scoreboards. This often leads to disputes regarding who buzzed first, human timing error, and slow scoring pace that interrupts the event flow.

WHAT I LEARNED

Developing Church Quiz challenged me to solve millisecond-accuracy race conditions across connected mobile devices, design high-contrast dark UI for projection screens, and build an intuitive moderator panel that requires zero training to operate during high-energy church events.
